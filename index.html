How it works

Unseal, place, sell, prestige.

That is the whole game at small scale. Everything else on the server makes that loop faster.

  1. 01

    Unseal

    Buy a Sealed Machine in /shop and right click it. New players get a free Tier 3.

  2. 02

    Place

    Set it on a square of your plot. /plot takes you home.

  3. 03

    Sell

    Each machine makes candy that sells itself every three seconds. More machines, higher tiers, more money.

  4. 04

    Prestige

    Upgrade, save up, and /prestige. Then do it again, bigger.

New here? The tutorial walks you through all of it and pays $10k when you finish. /guide and /faq cover the rest.

Your plot

25 squares. One machine on each.

Your plot is a 5 by 5 grid on a black and white tile floor. You start with one free square. Prestiging earns unlock credits, one every 25 Prestiges to begin with, and you pick which square opens next in /plot pick.

Store ranks add a head start: Baker +1, Chef +2, Pro +3, Master +4 bonus squares. Fill all 25 and your plot is maxed.

Past that, /vm opens at Prestige 500 with up to 20 Virtual Machines that need no squares at all.

  • Starting square
  • Rank bonus, up to 4
  • Unlocked with Prestige credits

Illustration. Square positions are set per plot.

Machines

20,000 tiers, every one a candy.

Sealed machines open with a rarity and four rolled stats: Speed, Yield, Luck and Sell. Right click a placed machine to upgrade it. The name changes every tier, from Sugar Dust to Hallowed Taffy at Tier 650, then starts over as Sugar Dust II.

You can see progress on your plot: the glass changes color with the tier and the whole machine grows up to Tier 550. The stove turning inside changes every 20 tiers up to Tier 81. Every 3 seconds the honey tray fills, a candy in the tier color pops out of the top, and Rare and better machines glow with their rarity bits orbiting around them.

Ways to earn

Farm for Tokens. Mine for the Forge.

The Farm /farm

Fifteen crops, from Sugar Wheat to Butterscotch Horn, unlock as you Prestige. Every harvest pays Tokens, and Tokens buy hoe enchants. The server's own tip: level Harvest and Scholar first.

P0P25P50P75P100P125

Crop unlocks by Prestige, 0 to 125.

Your own Farm Boss /boss

Nobody else can hit it. Every crop you harvest chips its health, and it drops Boss Shards for seven permanent upgrades, from Boss Damage to Boss XP.

The Mine /mine

Opens at Prestige 100. 15 ores, from Coal Ore to Respawn Anchor, drop Forge components and Golden Tickets. Forge components into Machine Parts and install them to boost your machines.

Features

More ways to play.

Candy Pond

/pond

Cast for 60 kinds of fish across 7 rarities, from tiny ones to Titanic. Keep the heaviest in your Aquarium: their weight boosts your Money, Tokens and XP, up to +1,250%.

  • 20 rod tiers and Auto Fish
  • Fish Index rewards
  • Merge fish to make them heavier

Opens at Prestige 250

Hoe Abilities

Hoe Enchants

Farm to charge your hoe, then press F for 10 seconds of chaos. Level each one up to 5 with Gems.

  • Harvest Storm: TNT that clears every crop around you
  • Golden Hour: Tokens up to x5
  • Lucky Streak: finders up to x4

Open at Prestige 0, 150 and 300

Mayor

/mayor

Three candidates, a new election every 16 hours. Vote with Tickets. The winner gives everyone +20% to their stat, a perk for the whole term and a surprise 5 minute x2 event.

  • Cheaper Machine Rolls, faster ability charge or more rare fish
  • Back the winner: double your Tickets back + 5 Gems

Everyone can vote

Halloween Event

/halloween

Find Candy Corn just by playing, then spend it in the Halloween Shop. Fight the Pumpkin King, hunt the hidden witch heads and finish the daily Trick, Treat and Haunt quests.

  • Jack o' Pet: x5 to every stat
  • Spooky tag, Gems and x2 boosts
  • Mythic Keys and Geodes for full days

On all season
